title: "Creamy Mushroom Risotto"
description: "Rich and creamy Italian risotto with sautéed mushrooms. A comforting dish that's perfect for a cozy dinner."

ingredients:
  - "1 1/2 cups Arborio rice"
  - "4 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warm"
  - "1 lb mixed mushrooms, sliced"
  - "1 small onion, diced"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1/2 cup white wine"
  - "1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "3 tbsp butter"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish"
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 30
servings: 4
category: "Main Course"

  - "risotto"
  - "mushroom"

  - "vegetarian"

1. Heat broth in a saucepan and keep warm over low heat.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
3. Add mushrooms and cook until golden, about 8 minutes. Remove and set aside.
4. Add butter to the same pan, then add onion and cook until translucent.
5. Add garlic and rice, stir for 2 minutes until rice is coated.
6. Add wine and stir until absorbed.
7. Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly until absorbed before adding more.
8. Continue until rice is creamy and al dente, about 20 minutes.
9. Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and season with salt and pepper.
10. Garnish with parsley and serve immediately.
