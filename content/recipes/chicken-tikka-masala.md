title: "Chicken Tikka Masala"
description: "Tender chicken in a rich, creamy tomato-based curry sauce. A popular Indian dish that's full of flavor."

ingredients:
  - "2 lbs chicken breast, cut into chunks"
  - "1 cup plain yogurt"
  - "2 tbsp lemon juice"
  - "2 tsp ground cumin"
  - "2 tsp ground coriander"
  - "2 tsp paprika"
  - "1 tsp turmeric"
  - "1 can (14 oz) tomato sauce"
  - "1 cup heavy cream"
  - "1 onion, diced"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated"
  - "2 tbsp butter"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh cilantro for garnish"
  - "Cooked basmati rice for serving"
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 25
servings: 6
category: "Main Course"
tags:
  - "chicken"
  - "indian"
  - "curry"
  - "spicy"
author: "Chef Rowley"
createdAt: "2024-02-01"
---

1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, lemon juice, and half the spices for at least 1 hour.
2. Heat butter in a large pan over medium heat.
3. Add onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
4. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute.
5. Add remaining spices and cook for 30 seconds.
6. Add tomato sauce and bring to a simmer.
7. Add marinated chicken and cook until chicken is done, about 15 minutes.
8. Stir in heavy cream and simmer for 5 minutes.
9. Season with salt and pepper.
10. Serve over basmati rice and garnish with cilantro.
