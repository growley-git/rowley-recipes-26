ingredients:
  - "3 ripe bananas, mashed"
  - "1/3 cup butter, melted"
  - "3/4 cup sugar"
  - "1 egg, beaten"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 tsp baking soda"
  - "Pinch of salt"
  - "1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)"
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 60
servings: 8

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Grease a 4x8 inch loaf pan.
3. In a large bowl, mix mashed bananas and melted butter.
4. Stir in sugar, egg, and vanilla extract.
5. Mix in baking soda and salt.
6. Stir in flour until just combined.
7. Fold in walnuts if using.
8. Pour batter into prepared loaf pan.
9. Bake for 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean.
10. Cool in pan for 10 minutes, then remove to wire rack to cool completely.
