ingredients:
  - "1 lb pizza dough"
  - "1/2 cup tomato sauce"
  - "8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced"
  - "Fresh basil leaves"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Cornmeal for dusting"
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 15
servings: 4

1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C) with pizza stone if available.
2. Roll out pizza dough on a floured surface.
3. Transfer to pizza pan or stone dusted with cornmeal.
4. Spread tomato sauce evenly over dough.
5. Top with mozzarella slices.
6. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with garlic.
7. Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
8. Remove from oven and top with fresh basil.
9. Season with salt and pepper, slice and serve.
