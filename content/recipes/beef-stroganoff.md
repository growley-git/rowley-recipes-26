ingredients:
  - "1 1/2 lbs beef sirloin, cut into strips"
  - "8 oz egg noodles"
  - "1 lb mushrooms, sliced"
  - "1 onion, sliced"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "2 cups beef broth"
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "3 tbsp all-purpose flour"
  - "2 tbsp butter"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 tsp Worcestershire sauce"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish"
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 25
servings: 6

1. Cook egg noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
2. Season beef strips with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over high heat.
4. Sear beef strips until browned, about 3-4 minutes. Remove and set aside.
5. Add butter to the same pan, then add onions and cook until soft.
6. Add mushrooms and garlic, cook until mushrooms are golden.
7. Sprinkle flour over vegetables and stir for 1 minute.
8. Gradually add beef broth, stirring constantly.
9. Add Worcestershire sauce and bring to a simmer.
10. Return beef to pan and simmer for 5 minutes.
11. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream.
12. Serve over egg noodles and garnish with parsley.
