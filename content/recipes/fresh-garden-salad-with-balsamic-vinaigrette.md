title: "Fresh Garden Salad with Balsamic Vinaigrette"
description: "A crisp, refreshing salad loaded with fresh vegetables and a tangy balsamic vinaigrette dressing."

ingredients:
  - "6 cups mixed greens (lettuce, spinach, arugula)"
  - "1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "1 cucumber, sliced"
  - "1 bell pepper, diced"
  - "1/2 red onion, thinly sliced"
  - "1/4 cup feta cheese, crumbled"
  - "1/4 cup walnuts, chopped"
  - "1/4 cup olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp balsamic vinegar"
  - "1 tsp Dijon mustard"
  - "1 tsp honey"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 0
servings: 4
category: "Salad"

  - "salad"
  - "healthy"
  - "vegetarian"
  - "fresh"
author: "Chef Rowley"
createdAt: "2024-01-22"
---

1. Wash and dry all vegetables thoroughly.
2. Combine mixed greens, tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, and red onion in a large bowl.
3. Whisk together olive oil, balsamic vinegar, Dijon mustard, and honey in a small bowl.
4. Season dressing with salt and pepper.
5. Toss salad with dressing just before serving.
6. Top with feta cheese and walnuts.
7. Serve immediately.
